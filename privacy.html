Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Welcome to 80 knee. This Privacy Policy explains how 80 knee ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, protects, and handles your personal information when you use our mobile application and web platform.

1. Information We Collect

To provide messaging, social feed, and telecom services, we collect:

  • Account Information: Your phone number, email address, display name/nickname, and profile bio.
  • User Profile Photos & Chat Media: Profile images and photos/videos you choose to send directly in private 1-on-1 messages.
  • Contacts Information: With your explicit on-device consent, we access phonebook contacts using the native device Contact Picker solely to identify which of your contacts are already registered on 80 knee. We do not store your entire address book on our servers or sell your contact list to third parties.
  • Transaction Data: Records of airtime and data purchases, network operator selected, recipient phone number, transaction references, and timestamps.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your identity and keep your account secure.
  • To enable private 1-on-1 direct messaging with friends saved in your contacts.
  • To filter your social feed so you only see posts shared by people in your contacts list.
  • To process automated airtime and data recharges to requested phone numbers.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with trusted industry providers to process payments and deliver telecom services:

  • Google Firebase: Used for secure cloud authentication, user profile storage, and real-time encrypted messaging.
  • Paystack (PCI-DSS Certified): Used to handle payments via card, bank transfer, and USSD. 80 knee does not store your debit card numbers, CVV, or banking PINs on its servers.
  • VTPass Telecom Gateway: Used to fulfill airtime and internet data bundle recharges directly to telecom operators (MTN, Airtel, Glo, 9mobile).

4. Data Retention & Security

We implement strict encryption protocols to safeguard your personal data, transaction records, and communication history against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.

5. Account & Data Deletion (Your Rights)

In full compliance with Google Play Store User Data policies, users have the right to request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal data at any time.

To delete your account and remove your profile photo, posts, messages, and phone number from our cloud database:

  • Use the in-app Logout / Account Management feature, or
  • Send an email request to support@80knee.com with your registered phone number. Your data will be permanently wiped within 7 business days.

6. Children's Privacy

80 knee is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
